# Changelog

## 2.0.0
Adds `revision_2026/`: analyses run in response to peer review (validation with training-only preprocessing and buffers of 5 and 10 km, fold maps and separation diagnostics, road-layer composition, equivalent road classes, effects in fixed units, sensitivity to building type, bootstrap stability), with their scripts, results, tables and figures. README, CHANGELOG and CITATION.cff were updated; nothing else of version 1.0.0 was changed.

## 1.0.0
First public release. It contains the data-inventory, quality-control and preprocessing stages, the presence-background point-process model with spatial block
cross-validation and block-bootstrap intervals, the tests, the reconstructed preparation scripts and the derived tables, metrics and figures of the article.
